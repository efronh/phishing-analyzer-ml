# Hold-out test: 2025 data the model never saw

Generated by `evaluate_holdout.py`. The saved model (`train.py`, trained on `kaggle.csv`, `nazario_sa.csv`, `modern_legit.csv`, `nazario_older.csv`) is used exactly as shipped. Nothing, including the threshold, is tuned on this data.

- **Phishing:** 454 emails from the Nazario corpus, 2025. That year is not in the training data.
- **Legitimate, set A:** 1478 emails from 8 Apache Software Foundation mailing lists (Feb, May and Aug 2025): user questions, developer discussion, release announcements.
- **Legitimate, set B:** 917 emails from Ubuntu mailing lists (Jan, Apr, Jul and Oct 2025): security notices, user questions, announcements.
- Emails that appear word-for-word in the training data are removed.
- **Set C, separate sources:** 214 English phishing emails from Phishing Pot (honeypot samples, 2026, CC BY-NC 4.0) and 767 legitimate emails from OSGeo mailing lists (2025).

## Excluding repeats of known campaigns

4 of the 454 phishing emails are near-copies (TF-IDF cosine ≥ 0.8) of 2023–24 phishing used in training. Same campaign, new ID. ML recall on those: 100.0%. On the 450 genuinely new ones: **99.1%** (rules: 30.2%).
