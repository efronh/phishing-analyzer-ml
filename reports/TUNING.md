# Near-duplicates and hyperparameters

Generated by `experiments_tuning.py`. The hold-out sets are not used for any choice made here.

## 1. How much did near-duplicates inflate the in-domain score?

Emails with TF-IDF cosine similarity ≥ 0.8 are put in one group: replies quoting each other, or the same campaign with a new ID. **5719 of 21506 training emails (27%)** have at least one near-copy. With a random split, copies of the same email can land in both train and test.

| model | precision | recall | f1 | fpr | roc_auc |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| random split | 0.9817 | 0.9825 | 0.9821 | 0.0109 | 0.9986 |
| near-duplicate groups kept together | 0.9753 | 0.9867 | 0.9810 | 0.0148 | 0.9984 |
