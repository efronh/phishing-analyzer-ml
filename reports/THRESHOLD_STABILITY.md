# Threshold stability

Generated by `threshold_stability.py`. The production configuration is trained with 5 seeds (different train/val/test splits and CV folds) and three ways of choosing the threshold:

- **val:** train on 70%, pick the max-F1 threshold on one 15% validation split.
- **cv-max:** 5-fold cross-validation on the other 85%, then take the peak of the mean F1 curve. As in production, the folds keep near-duplicate groups together and the scores are Platt-calibrated first.
- **cv-plateau:** the same CV, then take the middle of the flat top around the peak. The flat top is every adjacent threshold whose mean F1 is within one standard error (across folds) of the peak.

cv-max and cv-plateau share the same fold models and the same retrained model, so the only difference between them is the selection rule. The success criteria were set before running: the plateau rule should give a smaller threshold spread than cv-max, and the spread of the hold-out results should not grow. The hold-out sets are only measured here.
