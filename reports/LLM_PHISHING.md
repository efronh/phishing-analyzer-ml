# LLM-written phishing

Generated by `experiments_llm.py`. Protocol, written before the first run: [`LLM_PHISHING_PROTOCOL.md`](LLM_PHISHING_PROTOCOL.md). The saved production model at its own threshold (0.519); nothing is trained or tuned on these sets.

## Decision

Zenodo recall **97.1%** against 93.0% on hold-out C's human-written phishing (Fisher p = 0.00332): **LLM-written phishing is not harder for the model** under the pre-registered rule.
